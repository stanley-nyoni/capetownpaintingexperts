How We Work

Our Garden Service Process

Whether you need a once-off cleanup or a regular maintenance contract, we follow a thorough process to ensure your garden always looks its best.

1
Garden Assessment

We visit the property to assess the garden's current condition, identify problem areas and understand your maintenance goals.

2
Service Plan & Quote

We propose a tailored service plan — once-off visit or ongoing schedule — with transparent, itemised pricing. No hidden fees.

3
Initial Cleanup

Overgrowth cleared, lawn edged, beds weeded and all refuse removed from site. We establish a clean baseline to work from.

4
Regular Maintenance Visits

Scheduled visits keep the garden trimmed, mowed and looking beautiful. The same team visits every time — they know your garden.

5
Seasonal Care & Enrichment

We plant seasonally, fertilise, mulch beds and manage irrigation to keep your garden healthy and vibrant all year long.
