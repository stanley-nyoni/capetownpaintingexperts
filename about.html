Our Story

Built on Experience,
Driven by Excellence

Cape Town Painting Experts was founded with a simple mission: to deliver professional, reliable, and affordable property services to the people of Stellenbosch and Cape Town.

Over the past 12+ years, we've completed more than 450 projects from a simple repaint to full home renovations. Every job is handled with the same level of care and commitment to quality, regardless of size.

We are proud of our reputation. Our clients return to us because they trust us and that trust is something we work hard to earn and keep.
