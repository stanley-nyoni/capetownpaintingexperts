Our Process

How We Handle
Your Renovation

We plan every renovation carefully to minimise disruption and maximise your satisfaction with the end result.

1
Design Consultation

We sit down with you to understand your vision, needs, and budget for the renovation.

2
Detailed Quotation

A full, itemised quote is provided so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs.

3
Demolition & Prep

Old materials are removed, surfaces are prepared, and the space is made ready for transformation.

4

5
Final Handover

We clean up, do a full walkthrough with you, and hand over your beautifully transformed space.
