Our Process

How We Lay
Your Paving

A proper paving job starts long before the first brick is laid. Our process ensures a stable, beautiful result.

1
Site Assessment

We visit your property, measure the area, assess drainage needs, and provide a detailed quote.

2
Excavation & Ground Prep

We excavate to the correct depth, remove debris, and prepare a stable sub-base.

3
Base Layer

A compacted stone or sand base is laid to prevent sinking and ensure long-term stability.

4
Paving Installation

Bricks, pavers, or slabs are laid to your chosen pattern with precision and care.

5
Finishing & Sealing

Joints are filled, edges are finished, and a sealant is applied for protection and shine.
