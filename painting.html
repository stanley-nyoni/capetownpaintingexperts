How We Work

Our Painting Process

Every painting job follows our proven 5-step process to ensure quality, durability, and a finish you'll love for years.

1
Inspection & Quote

We assess the surfaces, identify problem areas, and provide a detailed, transparent quote with no hidden costs.

2
Preparation

We protect floors, furniture, and fittings before moving to the surface. All areas are cleaned, degreased, and ready for treatment.

3
Repair & Filling

Cracks, holes, and damaged areas are patched with polyfiller, sanded smooth, and primed for the best possible base.

4
Painting

We apply premium paint in 2–3 coats using the best rollers, brushes, and techniques for an even, long-lasting finish.

5
Final Touches & Inspection

We clean up, do a thorough walk-through with you, and fix any touch-ups before handing over. 100% satisfaction guaranteed.
